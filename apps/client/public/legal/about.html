How to play

  • Steer with your mouse (desktop) or the virtual joystick (mobile).
  • Boost by holding click / space / the boost button — it burns mass, so use it to strike or escape.
  • Eat candy to grow. Rarer candy is worth more. Donuts are jackpots.
  • Kill by making other worms crash into your body — never lead with your head.
  • Loot the glowing orbs a fallen worm leaves behind — and expect company.
  • Grab powerups: Speed, Magnet, Double Growth, Shield, Boost Discount, Score ×2.

Features

  • Instant play — no account, no install, free.
  • Real-time multiplayer with up to 40 worms per arena.
  • Six candy skins, live leaderboard, reconnect protection.
  • Works on desktop and mobile; installable as an app (PWA).
