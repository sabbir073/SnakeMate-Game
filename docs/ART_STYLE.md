# ART STYLE — the Nibblio look

**Theme:** candy-space. Bright glossy sweets drifting in a deep-purple void.

## Palette

Ground: `#12082B` (page/void), `#160B33` (world tile), `#1D0F42` (panels),
`#3A2477` (borders). Outline everywhere: `#2B1A3D`.
Candy accents (also the skin bases): mango `#FFB545`, bubblegum `#FF6FB5`,
mint `#58E6B4`, sky `#64D2FF`, grape `#9D6BFF`, lemon `#FFE066`.
Highlights: gold `#FFD166`; danger/border: `#E84393`.

## Shape language

Soft rounded geometry only — circles, capsules, rounded hexes. Bold dark
outline at ~5.5% of sprite size. Every filled shape gets: (1) a subtle radial
gradient, light source top-centre, (2) a glossy top-left highlight ellipse at
55% white. No hard corners, no thin lines, no texture noise.

## Worms

Head = candy ball + two big googly eyes (white ball, oversized pupil, sparkle)
looking along the heading, small open mouth at the very front. Face points
RIGHT in source art (engine angle 0). Body = candy ball with a half-shade and
an accent core dot; segments taper 35% toward the tail. Skins recolor
base/shade/accent only (data in packages/config/src/skins.ts) — silhouette
never changes, so readability is constant.

## Food

Distinct silhouettes per rarity so value reads at a glance: COMMON sky drop,
RARE mint wrapped candy, EPIC pink star, BONUS lemon-glazed donut with
sprinkles, DEATH_LOOT glowing amber orb with halo.

## Powerups

Rounded hex badge, per-kind gradient, white glyph with dark outline. Glyphs:
bolt / magnet / double-up-arrows / shield / flame / ×-star.

## Typography

Baloo 2 (OFL, self-hosted) for ALL dynamic text — never baked into art.
Wordmark = Baloo 2 ExtraBold outlines, gold→bubblegum vertical gradient,
30px dark outline.

## Effects & motion

Procedural in-engine only (spec §42): gentle food bob (±8%), powerup pulse
(±12%) and sway, boost tint (`#FFF1C9`) on worm sprites, shield ring stroke
`#8BD3FF`. All decorative motion is disabled by low quality / reduced-motion.

## Rules for future assets

1. Generated by `tools/asset-pipeline` from parametric SVG — never hand-pasted
   binaries. 2. Test at real gameplay size (worm 28–80px on screen, food
   14–32px). 3. New skins = new palette entry, nothing else. 4. Respect the
   outline + gloss recipe or it will look pasted-in.
